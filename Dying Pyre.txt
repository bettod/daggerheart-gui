PART 1
FRONT MATTER
 
THE FADING PYRE
An Age of Umbra Quickstart Adventure
For Tier 1 Heroes
When the last fire dies, what remains is not darkness.
It is memory.
 
Adventure Summary
The village of Ashgrove Hollow survives beneath the Sacred Pyre of Vaelor.
For centuries the flame has protected the settlement from the dangers of the Umbra.
Now it is failing.
Strange lantern lights have appeared within the forests surrounding the village. Ancient shrines have been disturbed. Long-forgotten guardians have returned from beneath the earth.
As the heroes investigate, they discover that the Sacred Pyre has never generated its own power.
Its light comes from a living spirit hidden below the valley.
A spirit known as Vaelor's Last Spark.
And that spirit has begun waking up.
The heroes must decide whether to save the flame, free the spirit, or choose an entirely new future.
 
Running This Adventure
The Fading Pyre is designed as a Tier 1 Daggerheart adventure for three to five player characters.
The adventure is organized into five acts.
Act
	Focus
	Act I
	Social Interaction
	Act II
	Combat
	Act III
	Investigation
	Act IV
	Exploration
	Act V
	Finale & Choice
	The story gradually introduces new aspects of play.
Act I introduces social interaction and Hope and Fear.
Act II introduces combat.
Acts III and IV emphasize investigation and discovery.
Act V combines everything learned so far into a climactic encounter and campaign-defining decision.
 
Themes
The Fading Pyre is built around four central themes.
Hope
The Age of Umbra is a world where hope matters because darkness is real.
The players should frequently encounter situations where courage and kindness are meaningful choices.
 
Community
Ashgrove Hollow is not a kingdom.
It is not a city.
It is a small settlement full of ordinary people trying to survive.
The players are fighting for their neighbors, not for glory.
 
Memory
Throughout the adventure, memories appear repeatedly:
·       Ash Wraiths consume them.
·       Vaelor's Last Spark preserves them.
·       The Sacred Pyre is built upon them.
Memory should feel like a form of power.
 
Sacrifice
Every major faction believes sacrifices are necessary.
The adventure asks whether certain sacrifices are justified.
There is deliberately no perfectly correct answer.
 
Welcome To The Age Of Umbra
No one remembers true daylight.
The oldest stories speak of a time when sunlight touched every corner of the world. Whether those stories are true no longer matters.
What matters is the darkness.
Across the Age of Umbra, isolated communities survive around fragile sources of light:
·       Sacred fires
·       Ancient beacons
·       Enchanted lanterns
·       Forgotten miracles
Every one of them is fading.
Ashgrove Hollow is no different.
For generations its people have survived beneath the light of the Sacred Pyre.
Now that fire weakens.
And if it dies, something waiting beyond the light may finally be allowed inside.
 
Adventure Overview
Act I
The Vigil Fire
The heroes arrive during a village-wide vigil.
The Sacred Pyre visibly weakens before their eyes.
They meet Keeper Elira Ashmantle and begin investigating the disaster.
 
Act II
Ashbound Raiders
The emergency emberwood convoy is attacked.
The heroes encounter desperate survivors from another community whose sacred flame has already died.
 
Act III
Seeking The Keeper
The investigation expands.
The heroes uncover the history of the Emberbound and begin piecing together the truth behind the Pyre.
 
Act IV
The Emberways
Ancient tunnels beneath the valley reveal Vaelor's Last Spark and the hidden origins of Ashgrove Hollow's survival.
 
Act V
Rekindling The Pyre
The heroes defend a ritual, confront the Gloam Shepherd, and decide the future of Ashgrove Hollow.

Arrival At Ashgrove Hollow
Read Aloud
The world does not know true morning.
Dawn and dusk have become difficult to distinguish.
Yet even in perpetual twilight, Ashgrove Hollow should be easy to find.
For generations travelers have followed the light of the Sacred Pyre.
Tonight that light struggles to reach the road.
Ahead, hundreds of villagers stand silently around a massive iron brazier.
No one speaks.
No one sings.
No one looks away.
They are all watching the fire.
Pause here.
Allow the players to introduce their characters.
Ask them how they arrived in Ashgrove Hollow and what first drew their attention.
 
Building The Village Together
Ask one question to each player.
Examples:
Ranger
What sign visible from outside the village tells you something is wrong?
Guardian
What tradition surrounding sacred fires did your people teach you?
Rogue
What detail tells you this crowd is frightened?
Sorcerer

Use every answer.
Incorporate them directly into Ashgrove Hollow.
 
The Sacred Pyre
The Sacred Pyre dominates the village square.
Even weakened, its flames climb nearly thirty feet into the air.
Silver sparks drift constantly upward and vanish somewhere in the darkness above.
The fire burns with white-gold light.
Characters may investigate.
 
Knowledge Roll (Difficulty 13)
Success:

Older than the village itself.
Success With Hope

Success With Fear
Gain Fear.

Only for a moment.
 
Instinct Roll (Difficulty 13)
Success:
The crowd is not praying.
They are waiting.
This has happened before.
Critical Success
The character notices something more disturbing.
The problem is not fuel.
The problem is the fire itself.
 
ACT I - PART 2
The Crying Child
As the players continue observing the vigil, introduce the first tutorial action roll.
Read Aloud
A child near the edge of the crowd suddenly begins to cry.
His mother kneels beside him, trying desperately to calm him.
The boy points toward the Sacred Pyre.
"It's getting smaller."
The words spread through the crowd.
Several villagers turn toward the flame.
Others glance nervously into the surrounding darkness.
The atmosphere grows noticeably more tense.
Ask the players:
What do you do?
Any reasonable attempt to comfort the child, encourage the crowd, distract attention, or reduce panic should trigger an Action Roll.
Presence Roll (Difficulty 10)
 
Success
The child calms.
The crowd relaxes slightly.
Success With Hope
The acting character gains 1 Hope.
Read aloud:
The boy slowly stops crying.
His breathing steadies.
A few nearby villagers smile weakly.
The fear remains.
But for a brief moment, hope feels stronger.
 
Success With Fear
The acting character succeeds, but a complication emerges.
Gain 1 Fear.
Read aloud:
The child quiets.
But before looking away from the forest he whispers:
"Something is watching."
Nobody else seems to have heard him.
Yet the feeling lingers.
 
Failure
The child remains frightened.
The crowd becomes increasingly uneasy.
Failure With Hope
The acting character gains 1 Hope.
Read aloud:
The child cannot be calmed.
Yet the attempt matters.
The villagers appreciate seeing someone try.
 
Failure With Fear
Gain 1 Fear.
The acting character marks 1 Stress.
Read aloud:
The child recoils.
Panic spreads through the gathered crowd.
For several moments the square fills with frightened whispers.
 
Critical Success
If both Duality Dice match:
The child immediately calms.
The player gains Hope and may clear Stress.
Read aloud:
The child's breathing slows.
He looks up at you.
Then toward the Sacred Pyre.
For just a moment the flame burns brighter.
Several villagers gasp.
Whether coincidence or miracle, nobody can say.

The Convoy Problem
The village stores sacred emberwood several miles away.
The material is carefully harvested and used to sustain the Sacred Pyre.
Without it:
The fire's condition could become much worse.
If the convoy has failed to return:
Something has gone wrong.

ACT II

Purpose Of The Act
This act introduces:
·       Combat
·       Adversaries
·       Fear Actions
·       Tactical choices
·       Moral ambiguity
Expected play time:
45-60 Minutes
Unlike many introductory combats, the Ashbound are not straightforward villains.
The players should initially assume they are bandits.
By the end of the act, they should realize the situation is considerably more complicated.
 
The Journey
The road north winds through dense pine woods.
Though only a few miles from the village, the atmosphere feels very different.
Read Aloud
The road narrows quickly after leaving Ashgrove Hollow.
Pine trees crowd close to either side of the path.
Their branches form a dark canopy overhead.
The further you travel, the quieter the forest becomes.
No birds sing.
No insects chatter.
Even the wind seems reluctant to move through these woods.
Something has disturbed the wilderness.
And the wilderness has noticed.

SEEKING THE KEEPER

By the time the heroes begin Act III, they know:
·       The Sacred Pyre is failing.
·       The Ashbound are connected to the crisis.

·       The Emberbound still exist.

60-90 Minutes

What Elira Knows
The Emberbound
Long before the Age of Umbra reached its present state, an order of guardians known as the Emberbound traveled between sacred sites.
They maintained ancient roads.
Protected holy places.
Guarded sources of sacred fire.
Over time they disappeared.
At least, that is what history claims.
 
Concerning Saint Vaelor
According to local stories:
Vaelor carried one of the final embers from the age before endless twilight.
That ember became the Sacred Pyre.
Whether the story is literally true is unknown.
 
Elira's Doubt
Read Aloud:

"Protect the village."
"Nothing else mattered."
She stares toward the Sacred Pyre.
"But if someone suffers because of that flame..."
Her voice softens.
"Then perhaps I do not understand my own duty anymore."
"I love this village too much to save it through a lie."

Roll or choose:
1. Emberbound Records
Ancient journals documenting pilgrimages between sacred fires.
 
2. Route Map
A faded map showing several sacred flames throughout the region.
This foreshadows future campaigns.
 
3. Blessed Ash Pouch
A small cloth bag containing silver ash.
Once per session, gain advantage on a roll to resist fear.
 
4. Forgotten Hymn
A fragment of an ancient song honoring the First Dawn.
 
5. Silver Rune Tablet
A portable relic containing protective symbols.
 
6. Memory Crystal
A crystal containing one preserved memory.
Read Aloud
A light flickers within the crystal.
Suddenly you witness an ancient scene.
The Sacred Pyre burns so brightly it can be seen from mountaintops far beyond the valley.
Thousands stand around it.
Laughing.
Singing.
Hoping.
Any character viewing the memory gains 1 Hope.
 
The Emberbound Camp
Beyond the sanctuary lies the hidden refuge of the Emberbound.
Read Aloud
One by one, silver lanterns ignite throughout the darkness.
Figures emerge from hidden doorways and side passages.
Some watch from balconies carved into stone.
Others stand openly upon the path ahead.
None draw weapons.
Yet no one appears relaxed.
These people have spent generations hiding from the world above.
And you are the first outsiders many of them have ever seen.
 
Brother Corvin
Leader of the Emberbound.
 
Appearance
·       Burn scars across both forearms.
·       Silver lantern staff.
·       Layered ceremonial robes faded with age.
 
Personality
·       Calm.
·       Patient.
·       Intelligent.
·       Honest.
 
Goal
Free Vaelor's Last Spark.
 
Fear
That humanity has become incapable of surviving without exploiting the spirit.
 
Meeting Corvin
Read Aloud
The crowd parts.
A lantern-bearing man steps forward.
His expression contains neither hostility nor fear.
Only exhaustion.
"Welcome," he says.
"Though I wish circumstances were kinder."
His lantern illuminates symbols matching those found throughout the village.
"I am Brother Corvin."
 
The Truth About The Sacred Pyre
Corvin explains the history hidden from Ashgrove Hollow.
Read Aloud
"The Sacred Pyre does not create power."
The words echo through the chamber.
Several Emberbound lower their heads.
"It never has."
He raises his lantern.
"Its light comes from a spirit."
"A spirit we once protected."
"A spirit your village forgot."
 
Revelation One
The Pyre is not self-sustaining.
 
Revelation Two
Its power comes from an ancient entity.
 
Revelation Three
That entity is:
Vaelor's Last Spark
 
Revelation Four
The spirit has begun awakening.
As it awakens, it instinctively reclaims power that has been draining away for centuries.
The weakening Pyre is merely a symptom.
 
Corvin's Doubt
The module should never imply Corvin is obviously correct.
Read Aloud
Corvin kneels beside his lantern.
"Do not mistake me."
"I believe the spirit deserves freedom."
"But belief is not certainty."
His gaze drifts toward the deeper tunnels.
"I do not know what happens if it awakens."
"None of us do."
This moment is important.
The players should understand that every major faction is operating with incomplete knowledge.
 
The Moral Question
Corvin will eventually ask:
Read Aloud
"Imagine someone tore your heart apart."
"Then used the pieces to keep strangers alive."
"For a year."
"For a century."
"For a thousand years."
He leans upon his lantern staff.
"How long would you accept that suffering?"
"When would survival stop justifying the cost?"
Encourage discussion.
Reward meaningful roleplay with Hope.
 
The Silver River
Corvin eventually guides the party deeper into the Emberways.
Read Aloud
The next chamber contains a river.
Yet no water flows within it.
Instead, liquid silver flame moves silently through the darkness.
The cavern glows with cold light.
Reflections dance across stone walls.
Despite being made of fire, nothing burns.
 
Crossing The River
The route forward requires a group challenge.
Objective
Achieve:
4 Successes Before 3 Failures
Possible approaches include:
·       Agility
·       Finesse
·       Instinct
·       Creative solutions
·       Magic
 
Example Complications
Failures may result in:
·       Marking Stress
·       Becoming separated
·       Losing equipment
·       Attracting attention
The challenge should feel dangerous without becoming lethal.
 
Hall Of Guardians
After crossing the Silver River, the party reaches enormous bronze doors.
Read Aloud
Towering metal doors block the passage ahead.
Their surface is covered in ancient writing.
Flames.
Stars.
The same symbols repeated endlessly.
At the center stands a larger inscription:
"Guardian of the Last Spark."
"Keeper of Sacred Memory."
"Defender of the Sleeping Flame."
 
Knowledge Roll (Difficulty 10)
Success
The guardian beyond was created to protect Vaelor's Last Spark.

Motionless.
Watching.

ACT V
REKINDLING THE PYRE
Purpose Of The Act
This act serves as the climax of the adventure.
Everything the heroes have learned now collides:
·       The Sacred Pyre is failing.
·       Vaelor's Last Spark is awakening.
·       The Emberbound seek freedom.
·       Keeper Elira seeks preservation.
·       The Gloam Shepherd seeks acceptance of the coming darkness.
The heroes must determine not merely how to save Ashgrove Hollow, but what kind of future deserves to survive.
Expected play time:
90-120 Minutes
 
The Return To Ashgrove Hollow
The ascent from the Emberways should feel urgent.
Allow brief roleplay while the heroes travel.
Ask each player:
Guardian
What responsibility drives you forward despite your exhaustion?
Ranger
What sight are you afraid you might see when you reach the village?
Rogue
What secret have you learned that changes how you view Ashgrove Hollow?
Sorcerer
What part of the Last Spark's memories still haunts you?
Reward particularly meaningful answers with Hope.
 
Arrival
Read Aloud
The first thing you notice is the smoke.
Thick black smoke rises above the trees.
Then the bells.
Dozens of bells.
Ringing constantly.
Ashgrove Hollow is in chaos.
Villagers rush through the streets carrying lanterns, oil, water, and bundles of wood.
The Sacred Pyre burns lower than ever before.
White flames barely rise above the rim of the brazier.
And standing beside the dying fire is a lone figure dressed entirely in black.
 
The Gloam Shepherd
The heroes finally meet the architect behind the disturbance.
The Shepherd is not a madman.
He is not seeking power.
He is not attempting conquest.
He believes he is helping.
That makes him far more dangerous.
 
Appearance
Read Aloud
His robes resemble funeral garments darkened by centuries of ash.
A blackened wooden staff rests in his hands.
Shadows seem to crawl slowly across its surface.
He studies the Sacred Pyre quietly.
Then he turns toward you.
His expression is calm.
Almost sympathetic.
"You found the truth."
His smile never reaches his eyes.
"And yet you still came."
 
Personality
The Shepherd should never sound angry.
He speaks with:
·       patience
·       certainty
·       sadness
He genuinely believes resistance only prolongs suffering.
 
The Final Debate
Allow a lengthy conversation before the ritual begins.
This may become one of the strongest roleplay scenes in the entire adventure.
Award Hope frequently.
The Shepherd should challenge assumptions rather than threaten.
 
On The Sacred Pyre
"Your village calls the flame sacred."
He gestures toward the brazier.
"What would they call it if they knew the cost?"
 
On Hope
"Hope is beautiful."
"False hope is monstrous."
 
On Humanity
"Every generation insists it can defeat the darkness."
"Every generation eventually learns otherwise."
 
On Vaelor's Last Spark
"A prison remains a prison."
"Even when its walls are built from gratitude."
 
Challenging The Heroes
The Shepherd should directly address individual characters.
Guardian
"How many lives would you sacrifice to save the village?"
 
Ranger
"How many strangers are worth one friend?"
 
Rogue
"If survival required betrayal, would you refuse?"
 
Sorcerer
"If your power depended upon another's suffering, would you abandon it?"
 
No answer is wrong.
The purpose is reflection.
 
Keeper Elira Takes Action
Eventually the debate must end.
Read Aloud
Elira slams her staff into the stones.
Silver fire erupts across the ground.
Ancient runes ignite around the Sacred Pyre.
A powerful wind tears through the village square.
Embers explode into the sky.
For a brief moment the Sacred Pyre burns brighter than it has in days.
Then Elira raises her voice.
"Enough."
"The village deserves a future."
She looks toward the heroes.
"Help me decide what that future will be."
 
THE RITUAL BEGINS
The ritual and battle occur simultaneously.
Keeper Elira cannot protect herself and complete the ritual.
The heroes must protect her.
 
Ritual Countdown
Place a D8 on the table.
Set it to: 8
This represents the ritual's progress.
 
Decrease The Countdown
Whenever:
·       An Ash Wraith is defeated.
·       A major ritual action succeeds.
·       The party completes a ritual objective.
·       A meaningful sacrifice is made.
 
Increase The Countdown
Whenever:
·       Elira is struck by an attack.
·       Ritual circles are damaged.
·       Major Fear Actions succeed.
·       Catastrophic failures occur.
 
Countdown Milestones
Countdown 8-7
Read Aloud
Silver sparks begin rising from the Pyre.
The flame stabilizes slightly.
 
Countdown 6-5
Read Aloud
Villagers begin to believe survival may still be possible.
Each hero gains 1 Hope.
 
Countdown 4
Read Aloud
One of the ritual circles cracks.
The Gloam Shepherd finally moves.
The Shepherd joins combat.
 
Countdown 3-2
Read Aloud
Memories flood the village square.
Ghostly images appear among the flames.
Entire generations briefly return.
Each player witnesses one meaningful memory.
 
Countdown 1
Read Aloud
Reality bends.
Every flame burns white.
Every shadow stretches farther than it should.
Time itself feels uncertain.
Each hero gains 1 Hope.
The GM gains 1 Fear.
 
Countdown 0
The ritual concludes immediately.
Proceed to Ending Resolution.
 
Encounter Setup
Place:
Keeper Elira
At the center of the ritual circle.
Difficulty 11.
Elira does not track HP.
Damage against her instead affects the Countdown.
 
4 Charcoal Husks
Begin within Very Close range of the ritual.
 
2 Ash Wraiths
Begin at Far range.
 
The Shepherd initially remains outside combat.
Observing.
 
CHARCOAL HUSKS
Use the Charcoal Husk stat block from the Adversary Appendix.
Behavior
They:
·       Attack ritual circles.
·       Ignore fear.
·       Focus on disruption.
They are less concerned with survival than with destruction.
 
ASH WRAITHS
Use the Ash Wraith stat block from the Adversary Appendix.
Behavior
They attack:
·       Isolated heroes
·       Characters carrying Hope
·       Protectors defending Elira
Whenever an Ash Wraith uses Consume Memory, ask the player a question.
Examples:
Guardian
Who depends on you?
Ranger
What are you still searching for?
Rogue
What truth are you afraid to face?
Sorcerer
What memory do you refuse to lose?
The Wraith attempts to consume the answer.
 
The Sacred Pyre
Environment Feature
Sacred Flame
While within Close range:
Players gain advantage against fear and despair.
 
Flare Of Hope
Once per scene:
A character may spend 2 Hope.
The Sacred Pyre erupts.
All allies:
·       Clear 1 Stress
 
Unstable Conduit
Fear Action.
Spend Fear.
Silver fire explodes from damaged runes.
All creatures within Very Close range mark Stress.
 
The Shepherd Enters Combat
At Countdown 4:
Read Aloud
The Shepherd lowers his staff.
"Then let us discover which future deserves to survive."
Darkness spills outward.
Shadows detach from nearby walls.
The final battle begins.
 
Phase Two
Umbra Ascendant
When the Shepherd drops below half Health:
Read Aloud
Shadows climb his body.
Darkness wraps around his robes.
He slowly rises into the air.
His voice echoes across the square.
"You cannot stop the night."
"You can only decide how to meet it."
 
The Shepherd gains:
·       Flight
·       +1 ATK
·       Ignore terrain
·       Shadow duplication
 
The Ritual Ends
When the Countdown reaches zero:
Immediately stop combat.
Read Aloud
Every flame freezes.
Every shadow stills.
Every sound vanishes.
The entire village holds its breath.
Vaelor's Last Spark descends toward the Sacred Pyre.
Waiting.
Listening.
Hoping.
For a choice.
 
ENDING ONE
RENEW THE BOND
The heroes choose to preserve the existing relationship between the spirit and the Pyre.
Read Aloud
Silver fire floods the Sacred Pyre.
The flame rises.
Higher.
Brighter.
Stronger.
Once again Ashgrove Hollow stands beneath its light.
The village survives.
Consequences
·       Ashgrove Hollow is saved.
·       The Pyre is restored.
·       Vaelor remains bound.

Protecting sacred fires throughout the Age of Umbra.
 
ENDING TWO
FREE THE SPARK
The heroes choose freedom.
Read Aloud
The Sacred Pyre dies.
Darkness swallows the square.
Several villagers cry out.
Then thousands of tiny embers rise into the sky.
They drift across the horizon like stars.
Hope is no longer located in one place.
It now belongs to everyone.
Consequences
·       The spirit is freed.
·       The Pyre is gone.
·       New lights appear throughout the world.
Campaign Focus
Travel alongside awakening spirits.

A hero sacrifices part of themselves.
Read Aloud
The silver flame enters you.
Memory.
Heat.
Joy.
Grief.
Ten thousand lives burn within your soul.
The Sacred Pyre ignites once more.
And a piece of it will remain part of you forever.
Consequences
·       Village survives.
·       One hero becomes transformed.
·       Vaelor remains partially free.
Campaign Focus
A chosen-vessel story.
 
ENDING FOUR
THE LIVING COUNCIL
Requirements:
·       Corvin survives.
·       Elira survives.
·       Peace is negotiated.
Read Aloud
No chains are restored.
No prison remains.
No promise is broken.
Instead, a new covenant is born.
Consequences

·       Vaelor remains willingly.
·       A new generation of guardians emerges.

Politics, diplomacy, and alliance building.

ASHEN NIGHT
If the ritual catastrophically fails.
Read Aloud
The final connection shatters.
The Sacred Pyre goes dark.
This time...
no flame returns.
Consequences
·       Ashgrove Hollow becomes a refugee settlement.
·       Darkness advances.
·       Survival becomes paramount.
Campaign Focus
Rebuilding hope after catastrophe.

Ashgrove Hollow is a small settlement located in a narrow valley.
The village survives beneath the light of the Sacred Pyre of Vaelor.

The Sacred Pyre
The Pyre has burned for centuries.
Travelers use it as a beacon.
Children are blessed before it.
The dead are cremated within it.
Most villagers cannot imagine life without it.
 
Saint Vaelor
Local stories say Vaelor carried a single sacred ember through the darkness.
The Pyre was born from that ember.
Whether the story is literally true is unknown.
 
The Umbra
Every child grows up hearing warnings about the darkness beyond the village.
Nobody agrees about what the Umbra actually is.
Everybody agrees it is dangerous.
 
Recent Events

·       The Sacred Pyre has weakened.

CONNECTIONS
Before play, each player chooses one:
I trust you with my life.
You saved me once.
We disagree about what should be done.
I owe you a debt.
You remind me of someone I lost.
We survived a failed settlement together.
These fit the themes of The Fading Pyre much better than generic adventuring-party relationships.
This gives you a complete quickstart roster with a mix of:
·       Arcane caster
·       Frontline warrior
·       Rogue
·       Tank guardian
·       Ranged specialist
·       Spiritual support
·       Construct veteran
and ties every character directly to the central themes of sacred fires, memory, survival, and hope.
